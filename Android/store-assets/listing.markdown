## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Prosary is a companion for praying the Rosary and other Catholic devotions — built for the quiet, unhurried rhythm of devotional life, not the pace of a typical app.

PRAY THE ROSARY, YOUR WAY
• Today's Mysteries follows the traditional weekday assignment automatically, with the correct liturgical-season overrides on Sundays — or choose a specific set (Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, Luminous) yourself.
• Pray the traditional 15 mysteries, or all 20 in one sitting, in the chronological order of Christ's life.
• Choose traditional or Eastern mystery artwork, presenter mode, closing intentions, the Apostles' Creed, opening prayers, Fatima Prayer, Eternal Rest, St. Michael Prayer, and seasonal or fixed Marian antiphons.
• A bead-by-bead progress tracker keeps you oriented without ever feeling like a checklist.

TEN BUILT-IN DEVOTIONS
• Pray the Angelus, Stations of the Cross, Via Lucis, Divine Mercy Chaplet, Franciscan Crown, Seven Sorrows, Trisagion, and the seven-day O Antiphons devotion.
• Switch between alternate forms where a devotion offers them, and let multi-day devotions remember where you are.
• Pray the Jesus Prayer with a repetition target of 33, 66, 99, a custom count, or unbounded for open-ended contemplative prayer.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
The prayer catalog includes Latin, English, Arabic, Hebrew, Classical Syriac/Aramaic, Greek, Spanish, Russian, and Tagalog; each devotion lists the languages it supplies. Most built-ins cover Latin, English, Arabic, Hebrew, Russian, and Tagalog. Arabic, Hebrew, and Aramaic render right-to-left, and Hebrew includes the distinct wording used by the St James Vicariate and the Mission of St. Gamaliel.

YOUR PRAY LIST
Pin the devotions you pray, put them in your own order, and save named Rosary or Jesus Prayer configurations. Set local daily reminders, use hands-free auto-advance, and open the basic prayers on their own whenever you need them.

DISCOVER AND SHARE
Browse community devotions inside the app, search local and community prayers together, or group them by category. Prosary also imports open .prosaryprayer bundles authored with Prosary Compose.

BUILT FOR PRAYING TOGETHER
Prosary's clean, focused layout works well for leading a Rosary over a video call, or for a family or parish prayer group praying together from separate devices.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
The app has no account, analytics, ads, or tracking. Saved prayers and reminders stay in local app storage. Its only network feature is the optional community catalog: browsing/searching fetches a public index, and downloading a devotion increments that bundle's anonymous aggregate count. See prosary.app/privacy for details.

Prosary is free and open source.
