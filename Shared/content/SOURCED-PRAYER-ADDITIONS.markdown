# Further published prayer sources, 19 September 2026

These additions use published wording. The offline source fixture is
[`sourced-prayer-additions.json`](../tools/fixtures/sourced-prayer-additions.json),
checked by `Shared/tools/test-sourced-prayer-additions.py`.

## Greek Rosary opening

Both invitatory variants follow *Officium Parvum Graece et Latine ad usum
Congregationis B. Mariae Virginis* (Avignon: François Seguin, 1823),
[printed page 7](https://books.google.com/books?id=DY_8v-ZEfCYC&pg=PA7).
The scan was read visually. Polytonic spelling is retained, printed στ ligatures
expanded, line wrapping removed and the response emphasized for the app.
The Lenten variant omits the final Alleluia, as the canonical app prayer requires.
The book's alternative seasonal response is not added. This is a public-domain
printed source, already used for other Greek Rosary prayers.

## Greek Stations response

The [Catholic Bishops' Conference of Greece publication of 23 March 2020](https://cen.gr/δρόμος-του-σταυρού-του-2019-του-ι-καθολικο/)
reproduces the Stations used at the Catholic Cathedral of St Dionysius in 2019.
Only its repeated, 15-word traditional acclamation is quoted. The modern
meditations and different opening and closing prayers are not imported.
The fixture pins the displayed response by checksum without duplicating the excerpt.

## Spanish Seven Sorrows closing

The complete collect is transcribed from José Pulido y Espinosa's *Misal Romano*
(Madrid: Imprenta a cargo de Zacarías Soler, 1851),
[printed page 333](https://books.google.com/books?id=mE9Xg0g9l3kC&pg=PA333),
under the Mass for the Sorrows of Mary. The
[title page](https://books.google.com/books?id=mE9Xg0g9l3kC&pg=PA1)
and prayer page were inspected directly; their scan checksums are recorded.
This is a public-domain historical translation. Its vocabulary and spelling
are retained, including the translator's *Madre nuestra* and *por la muerte*
wording. Spaces and line-end hyphenation are normalized. The intercession of
all the saints at the Cross is present; modern shortened versions lacking this
clause were not substituted.

The preceding 14-word versicle and response are a short excerpt from
*Corona de los Dolores de María Santísima*, Apostolado Sacerdotal / Pierina
Devoções, [PDF page 4](https://pierinashop.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/corona-dolorosa.pdf#page=4),
visually checked against the publisher's leaflet. No other modern leaflet text
is copied. The conventional invitation *Oremos* is existing app wording.

## Further leads that did not supply reusable matching texts

- The Greek Leonine Michael prayer is published in
  [the TLIG Greek prayer booklet](https://tlig.org/languages/el/downloads/Prayerbooklet.pdf),
  PDF page 21 / printed page 16, but a historical translator or permission to
  reproduce the complete modern Greek translation has not been established.
- The official Greek Stations publication above supplies different opening and
  closing prayers from the app's intended texts. Those are not substitutes for
  the missing translations. A [Greek Via Lucis announcement](https://www.syros-agenda.gr/thriskeia-prosklisi-gia-neous-ypaithrios-dromos-tou-fotos/)
  confirms the devotion but does not publish its acclamation.
- The [Our Parish Priest article of 12 December 2022](https://ourparishpriest.blogspot.com/2022/12/ano-ang-o-antiphons-maiikling.html)
  supplies seven abbreviated Tagalog invocations, each ending before the full
  antiphon. Its permission to credit original content cannot supply the omitted
  prayer text. The commonly circulated *Halina, O Emmanuel* is a hymn paraphrase,
  not these seven complete prayers.
- A complete Tagalog musical setting was located in *Mga Awitin sa Panahon ng
  Adbiyento* (Consolacion Publications, October 2024), words credited to Dhong S.
  Zamora and music to Alejandro Consolacion II, pages 23–25. Its publication
  explicitly reserves all rights. The text has not been copied. The modern
  *Panalangin ng mga Kristiyano sa Maghapon* also credits the Tagalog liturgical
  commission; availability online does not establish reuse permission.

The unresolved Greek and Tagalog traditional prayers remain absent, with the
existing fallback behavior. These findings record the reviewed sources and
their limits; they do not assert that suitable translations cannot be found.
