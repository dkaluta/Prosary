## Boundary findings

- **Ephesians 1:1–10:** The original Hebrew publication and English corroboration end at gathering heavenly and earthly things in Christ. Target verse 10 covers that ending; the inheritance/election clause belongs to 11 and is not appended.
- **Ephesians 1:11–14:** The original Hebrew and corroborating English publications open with inheritance/election in Christ, covered by target verse 11. The gathering clause in verse 10 is not appended.
- **Ephesians 5:3–13:** The Maronite publication ends within verse 13, before the following light clause retained by all eight complete target verses. Preserve 3–13 with a notice; the awake/sleeper quotation in 14 is not appointed.
- **John 7:1–13:** All five current calendar contexts open with Jesus in Galilee and end with fear of speaking publicly. Official UGCC Scripture, the Royal Doors RSVCE reader, Missale Meum and the Syriac/Maronite publications establish those endpoints. The preceding Judas clause in 6:71/72 is not appended.
- **Luke 7:11–18:** The Syriac-calendar publication includes John summoning two disciples in its verse 18. All eight pinned target editions place that summons in 19. Include full verse 19, disclosing its additional sending and question clauses.
- **Mark 3:13–19:** Official UGCC and Syriac-calendar verse 19 end at the betrayal. Royal Doors explicitly selects RSVCE, whose verse 19 also includes returning home. The shared key uses a reviewed whole-verse envelope covering both endpoints: Tagalog/Peshitta already contain the return home in 19; the other six need 20, including its crowd/eating clauses.
- **Mark 3:20–27:** Official UGCC verse 20 includes returning home; Royal Doors RSVCE verse 20 starts with the crowd. The shared key covers both openings. Tagalog/Peshitta need full verse 19 for the return home, retaining the betrayal clause with a notice; the other six contain the return home in 20.
- **Mark 3:20–30:** The Syriac-calendar publication explicitly begins with returning home. Tagalog/Peshitta need full verse 19 to retain that opening. The other six begin at 20. The notice discloses the additional betrayal clause.
- **Revelation 12:1–12:** The Maronite publication and the five currently emitted editions finish with the devil having little time. Pursuit of the woman belongs to 13 and is not appended. This endpoint review does not relax any other edition exclusion.
- **Revelation 12:7–12:** The Maronite publication and the five currently emitted editions finish with the devil having little time. Pursuit of the woman belongs to 13 and is not appended. This endpoint review does not relax any other edition exclusion.
- **1 Peter 3:8–15:** The original Missale Meum Latin and English end within verse 15 after sanctifying Christ in the heart. Preserve the complete verse 15 with a notice. Verse 16 is not appended.
- **Acts 3:13–15; 3:17–19:** The original Missale Meum Latin and English end with sins being blotted out. Tagalog, Kulish and Peshitta verse 19 also include the refreshment clause. Preserve the original discontinuous full-verse sequence with a notice; do not append 20.
- **Mark 16:1–7:** The original Missale Meum Latin and English begin with the women buying spices after the liturgical incipit, omitting the Sabbath opening retained in all eight target verse 1 units. Preserve full verses 1–7 with a notice; do not append 8.

The additional Philippians probe produced 112 pairs. Ninety-one were false positives because its temporary predicate omitted the chapter-1 condition and included chapters 2–4. The remaining 21 valid pairs cover the three chapter-1 keys `1:12–20`, `1:12–21` and `1:12–30` across seven editions. Each already contains both source verses 16/17 and both Standard units 16/17. Their source-native verse order remains intact; no data change is needed.
