## Recent progress

- Wisdom 13–15 completed independent review and reconciliation. Chapter 16 completed both full comparisons, with three visible corrections and eleven exact vowel notes integrated; its damaged consonant remains unresolved. Chapter 17 gained a complete first draft and separate author comparison.
- First Maccabees advanced from source page B159 through B171, completing chapters 12–13 and chapter 14 through verse 47.
- Second Maccabees 6–12 completed reconciliation. Chapter 13 now has a complete first draft and separate author proof.
- Corrections preserve the printing's actual spelling, point placement, brackets, combined verse labels, and text crossing page boundaries.

Unreadable isolated vowel marks receive exact source notes. The notes identify the affected letter; readable neighboring marks remain. The approved restoration of ל in Wisdom 1:16 remains explicitly disclosed. A user decision is pending on whether to restore the candidate האזינו in Wisdom 6:2 with an editorial note; that reconstruction has not been applied or approved. In Wisdom 16:23, a tiny surviving stroke could be a yod or a damaged vav. The candidate remains confined to the unapproved draft; no consonant has been restored there.
