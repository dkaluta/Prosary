## Progress since the preceding pushed checkpoint

- Wisdom 10–12 completed independent review and reconciliation; chapters 11–13 gained saved drafts and complete author comparisons.
- First Maccabees advanced from source page B154 through B159, completing chapter 11 and the opening of chapter 12.
- Second Maccabees 4–5 completed reconciliation; chapters 6–8 advanced through author proofreading.

Unreadable isolated vowel marks receive exact source notes. The notes identify the affected letter; readable neighboring marks remain. The approved restoration of ל in Wisdom 1:16 remains explicitly disclosed. No new consonant reconstruction has been approved for Wisdom 6:2.
