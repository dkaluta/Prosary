## Recent progress

- Wisdom 13–15 completed independent review and reconciliation. Chapter 16 gained a full first draft and separate author comparison of all 28 source units, covering 29 printed verse labels.
- First Maccabees advanced from source page B159 through B168, completing chapters 12–13 and the opening of chapter 14.
- Second Maccabees 6–10 completed reconciliation. Chapter 11 has a full author proof, and chapter 12 now has a complete first draft.
- Corrections preserve the printing's actual spelling, point placement, brackets, combined verse labels, and text crossing page boundaries.

Unreadable isolated vowel marks receive exact source notes. The notes identify the affected letter; readable neighboring marks remain. The approved restoration of ל in Wisdom 1:16 remains explicitly disclosed. A user decision is pending on whether to restore the candidate האזינו in Wisdom 6:2 with an editorial note; that reconstruction has not been applied or approved.
