## Progress since the latest pushed checkpoint

- Wisdom 13 completed independent review and reconciliation. Chapter 14 gained a full draft and a separate author comparison of all 45 printed rows.
- First Maccabees advanced from source page B159 through B163, completing chapter 12 and the opening of chapter 13.
- Second Maccabees 6–8 completed reconciliation. Chapter 9 has a full author proof, and chapter 10 has a full first draft.
- Corrections preserve the printing's actual spelling, point placement, brackets, combined verse labels, and text crossing page boundaries.

Unreadable isolated vowel marks receive exact source notes. The notes identify the affected letter; readable neighboring marks remain. The approved restoration of ל in Wisdom 1:16 remains explicitly disclosed. No new consonant reconstruction has been approved for Wisdom 6:2.
