- Wisdom 13–15 and 17–19 completed independent review and reconciliation. Chapter 18's omitted word in 7–8 is restored from the visible print, and an extra draft letter in 14 is removed. Chapter 19's independent review restored a visible letter missed in the earlier pass and corrected its points; 22 exact point notes disclose what the scan cannot resolve.
- First Maccabees advanced from source page B159 through the final page, B177, completing the pointed transcription of all 16 chapters.
- Second Maccabees 6–15 completed reconciliation, apart from the damaged word in 14:4. The final chapter's independent review identified an unreadable compound vowel; only that mark is omitted, with an exact source note.
- Corrections preserve the printing's actual spelling, point placement, brackets, combined verse labels, and text crossing page boundaries.

Unreadable isolated vowel marks receive exact source notes. The notes identify the affected letter; readable neighboring marks remain. The approved restoration of ל in Wisdom 1:16 remains explicitly disclosed. A user decision is pending on whether to restore the candidate האזינו in Wisdom 6:2 with an editorial note; that reconstruction has not been applied or approved. In Wisdom 16:23, a tiny surviving stroke could be a yod or a damaged vav. In Second Maccabees 14:4, the year-word after “one hundred and fifty” is damaged in both available scans. These consonant questions remain open; provisional draft readings are not certified restorations.
