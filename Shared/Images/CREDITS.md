# Image credits

All artwork in this directory is served to every platform through `.prosaryprayer` content
bundles (see `../ARCHITECTURE.md`). Every file is a square (1:1) crop, centered on Christ where
he is present, otherwise on the scene's subject, prepared from the sources below. Crops and
brightness-normalization are the only modifications.

## Stations of the Cross (`station_01`–`station_14`)

All 14 from one coherent cycle: **Gebhard Fugel (1863–1939), Kreuzweg (1921), St. Antonius,
Bad Saulgau, Germany.** Paintings are public domain (artist died more than 80 years ago).
Photographs by Andreas Praefcke, released into the public domain by the photographer (PD-self).
Source files: Wikimedia Commons, `File:Saulgau_Antoniuskirche_Kreuzweg_Fugel_Station_NN.jpg`
(NN = 01…14), from
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Kreuzweg_in_St._Antonius_(Bad_Saulgau)>.

## Rosary mysteries and fixed-prayer illustrations

The 20 mystery paintings (`joyful_*`, `sorrowful_*`, `glorious_*`, `luminous_*`) and the 10
fixed-prayer illustrations (`crucifix`, `our_father`, `glory_be`, `jesus_portrait`,
`eternal_rest`, `madonna_and_child`, `st_michael`, `virtue_*`) predate this file; their
attributions are listed in the app's About screen. `cross_placeholder.png` is original to the
project.
