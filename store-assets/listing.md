## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Prosary is a companion for praying the Rosary and other Catholic devotions — built for the quiet, unhurried rhythm of a devotional life, not the pace of a typical app.

PRAY THE ROSARY, YOUR WAY
• Today's Mysteries follows the traditional weekday assignment automatically, with the correct liturgical-season overrides on Sundays — or choose a specific set (Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, Luminous) yourself.
• Pray the traditional 15 mysteries, or all 20 in one sitting, in the chronological order of Christ's life.
• Every classical option is here: the Apostles' Creed, the opening Our Father and three Hail Marys, the Fatima Prayer after each decade, the Eternal Rest prayer for the faithful departed, the St. Michael Prayer, and a seasonal or fixed Marian antiphon to close.
• A bead-by-bead progress tracker keeps you oriented without ever feeling like a checklist.

THE ANGELUS AND THE JESUS PRAYER
• Pray the Angelus, the Church's traditional threefold remembrance of the Incarnation.
• Pray the Jesus Prayer with a repetition target of 33, 66, 99, a custom count, or unbounded for open-ended contemplative prayer.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Pray in Latin, English, Arabic, Hebrew, Russian, or Tagalog — with full right-to-left support for Arabic and Hebrew, serving Holy Land Christian communities and beyond.

FAVORITES AND REMINDERS
Save any combination of settings as a named favorite — a morning Rosary in Latin, an evening Rosary for the departed, a Jesus Prayer of 100 repetitions — and set a daily reminder for each one. Reminders are scheduled entirely on your device.

BUILT FOR PRAYING TOGETHER
Prosary's clean, focused layout works well for leading a Rosary over a video call, or for a family or parish prayer group praying together from separate devices.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Prosary collects no data of any kind. There's no account, no analytics, no ads, and no network access at all — the app is architecturally incapable of sending or receiving data over the internet. Everything you save stays on your device.

Prosary is free and open source.
