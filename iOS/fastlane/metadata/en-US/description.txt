Prosary is a quiet companion for Catholic prayer — the daily Rosary and a whole treasury of devotions, presented beautifully and prayed at your pace.

PRAY THE CLASSICS
• The Holy Rosary, with the day's mysteries chosen for you and a bead track that follows your progress
• Stations of the Cross — traditional (St. Alphonsus) and scriptural forms
• Via Lucis, the Franciscan Crown, the Seven Sorrows, the Divine Mercy Chaplet, the Angelus, the Trisagion, and the Jesus Prayer

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Prayers in Latin, English, Hebrew and more — switch languages mid-prayer without losing your place. Scripture is set in a proper reading typeface, right to left where it belongs.

HANDS-FREE AND NARRATED
Auto-advance turns pages for you every few seconds, and devotions with narrated recordings follow the audio chapter by chapter — pause, skip, and resume where you left off.

THE COMMUNITY REPOSITORY
Browse and install devotions shared at prayers.prosary.app with one tap, and get updates when authors improve them. Compose your own at compose.prosary.app — no coding, everything in the browser — and publish it for everyone.

MULTI-DAY DEVOTIONS
Novenas and consecrations keep your place: finish today's day and tomorrow opens on the next.

QUIET BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Reminders are scheduled on your device. The only network feature is the optional community repository — and if you never open it, Prosary makes no network requests at all.

Today's feast and the Pope's monthly intention greet you each morning, per the calendar of the Latin Patriarchate of Jerusalem.
